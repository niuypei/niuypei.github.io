About Me

My research interests span computer systems and AI, including scalable large language model training, reinforcement learning, and multi-agent systems. I also study serverless and hybrid cloud computing, resource management, and networked systems.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Architecture from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in June 2023, advised by Prof. Fangming Liu.

Research Interests

  • LLM Training
  • Distributed Systems
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • Multi-Agent Systems
  • Serverless Computing
  • Cloud Resource Management
